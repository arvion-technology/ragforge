Score formula

IDF is computed once per term from document frequency. The second factor rewards term frequency, damped by chunk length.

idf = ln(1 + (N - df + 0.5) / (df + 0.5))
score = Σ idf · tf·(k1+1) / (tf + k1·(1 - b + b·len/avgdl))
